The **Compound Bow** does not require arrows, has infinite durability, and its arrows are unaffected by gravity.

Any enchantment can be applied to the Compound Bow with an Anvil, even if it is normally incompatible with the item or its other enchantments. Each enchanted book adds its level to the enchantment already on the Compound Bow, with no maximum level.
