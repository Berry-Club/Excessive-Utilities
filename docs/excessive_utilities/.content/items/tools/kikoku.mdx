The **Kikoku** is a powerful weapon that, in addition to dealing damage, **lowers the maximum health of the target**.

Any enchantment can be applied to the Kikoku with an Anvil, even if it is normally incompatible with the item or its other enchantments. Each enchanted book adds its level to the enchantment already on the Kikoku, with no maximum level.
