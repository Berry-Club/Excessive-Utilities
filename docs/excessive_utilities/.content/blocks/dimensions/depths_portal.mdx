**Portal to the Depths** is a block.

Use the Portal with an empty hand to enter **The Depths**, a cavern dimension made mostly from Deepslate. Vanilla ores generate more densely than they do in the Overworld, making the dimension useful for mining once you are prepared for its hazards.

The Depths also contains large caves, Deepslate pillars and stalactites, Sculk Veins, and occasional Ancient Cities. It is much darker than an ordinary Overworld cave, and that darkness is dangerous in its own right: by default, players below light level 4 take 1 damage every 5 seconds. Bring plenty of light sources and place them as you explore.

## Entering and Leaving

Each Portal creates or reuses a small arrival room in the Depths. The room contains another Portal; use it to return to the dimension and position of the Portal you entered through.

The mod remembers that return point on the player. If the return information is ever missing, using a Portal inside the Depths sends the player to the Overworld's shared spawn instead of leaving them trapped.

Portals whose arrival rooms would overlap may lead to the same room.

## Configuration

Server owners can configure the amount of darkness damage, how often it is dealt, and the minimum light level considered safe.
