The **Mechanical Miner** automatically breaks the block directly in front of it once per second. It simulates mining with a Diamond Pickaxe and collects the resulting drops in its internal inventory.

An Enchanted Book can be placed in the dedicated slot to apply its enchantments to the simulated pickaxe. For example, Fortune increases eligible drops, while Silk Touch collects eligible blocks themselves.

The Mechanical Miner has nine storage slots and has another for [Speed Upgrades]($content/items/upgrades/speed_upgrades) in its upgrade slot. Each Speed Upgrade makes it operate one additional time per second, but also increases its [Grid Power]($docs/players/grid_power).

Its redstone mode can be set to always operate, operate while powered, operate while unpowered, or operate once per redstone pulse.
